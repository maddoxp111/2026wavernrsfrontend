Claim your comps
The archive preserves comps from Reddit, Discord, untitled.stream, Samply and MEGA. If some of them are yours, here is how to get them under your name, on your account, or taken down.
1. Connect Reddit or Discord — your comps move to you automatically
Comps imported from Reddit are filed under your u/ name; comps from Discord are tied to your Discord account. Open Settings and connect Reddit (paste a short code into your Reddit profile so we know it's you) and/or Discord. Every comp and edit the archive has under those names moves onto your wavernrs profile on the spot. Nothing else to fill in.
2. Everything else — send a claim
For comps that came from Reddit, untitled.stream, Samply or MEGA, or that are listed under a name that isn't yours, use the form below. It goes straight to the archive team. Use it when:
  • Wrong name. Your comps show under a username or spelling you don't want. Tell us what to call the profile.
  • Move to my account. You want the comps on your wavernrs account so they show as your own uploads.
  • Someone posted my comp as theirs. A comp in the archive is yours but credited to someone else. We move it to you and note the correction.
  • Take it down. You made it and don't want it archived.
Proof matters most. The best proof is a link to the original post, project, or upload from an account we can see is yours, a screenshot of the project in your untitled.stream or Samply library, or a message from the account that originally posted it. Claims without proof are declined.
