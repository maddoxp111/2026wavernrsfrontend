Claim your comps
the archive saves comps from reddit, discord, untitled.stream, samply and MEGA. if some of them are yours, this is how you get them under your name, on your account, or taken down.
1. Connect Reddit or Discord — your comps move to you automatically
comps from reddit are under your u/ name and comps from discord are tied to your discord account. go to Settings and connect reddit (put a short code in your reddit bio or post it as a comment so we know its you) and/or discord. every comp and edit under those names moves to your wavernrs profile right away...nothing else to fill out.
2. Everything else — send a claim
for comps from reddit, untitled.stream, samply or MEGA, or ones listed under a name thats not yours, use the form below. it goes straight to the archive team. use it if:
  • Wrong name. your comps are under a name or spelling you dont want. tell us what the profile should be called.
  • Move to my account. you want the comps on your wavernrs account so they show up as your uploads.
  • Someone posted my comp as theirs. a comp in the archive is yours but someone else got the credit. well move it to you and note the fix.
  • Takedown request. you made it and dont want it archived, or its your stuff being hosted without your ok. pick "Takedown request" in the form and link the comp...the team takes it down after checking.
proof is the most important part. best proof is a link to the original post or upload from an account thats clearly yours, a screenshot of the project in your untitled.stream or samply library, or a message from the account that first posted it. claims with no proof get declined.
